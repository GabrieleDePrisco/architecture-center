## About this Document

This AI Native North Star Architecture document defines the target state of SAP’s technology landscape. It is not a specification, roadmap, or promise of delivery. It is a direction of travel; a statement of how AI, data, processes, and the platform must work together as intelligence becomes the norm rather than an add-on. This document is a natural evolution and extension to the **[North Star Architecture Strategy 2024 document](https://dam.sap.com/mac/app/p/pdf/asset/preview/uQ48mvp?h=&ltr=a)**.

The internal version of the document will live on the [OCTO GitHub](https://pages.github.tools.sap/OCTO/ai-native-northstar/) and evolve with the pace of AI itself. Updates will appear quarterly. Each revision is open to review, versioned, and traceable. Note that the document is for SAP-internal use only. We are working on an external version to share our architectural guidance with customers and partners as well.
